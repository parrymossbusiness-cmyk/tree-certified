Tree Service in West Sacramento, CA

Licensed, insured removal, trimming, and stump grinding for homeowners across the river in Yolo County.

West Sacramento sits just across the Sacramento River from the capital, and its established neighborhoods — Broderick, Bryte, Washington, and the areas around the river levee trail — carry some of the most mature tree canopy in the metro. Many homes here date back several decades, and the valley oaks and sycamores planted alongside them have grown large enough that routine maintenance now often means real structural work: crown reduction, deadwood removal, and occasional emergency response when a limb finally gives out.

Because West Sacramento's older neighborhoods were built before modern setback standards, it's common to find large trees growing just a few feet from a foundation or driveway, which changes how we approach both trimming and removal. Our crews plan rigging and drop zones carefully in these tighter lots, and we're familiar with the mix of housing stock across the city, from the historic homes near Broderick to newer construction further south toward the Southport area.

We serve all of West Sacramento and stay close to our neighbors in Davis, Woodland, and Sacramento, which lets us respond quickly to storm damage on either side of the river. Whether it's a single overgrown tree or a full-property assessment after severe weather, our team knows the neighborhoods and the trees in them.
