Tree Service in Citrus Heights, CA

Licensed, insured removal, trimming, and stump grinding for homeowners across Sacramento County.

Citrus Heights sits along Interstate 80 northeast of Sacramento and combines older neighborhoods near Sylvan Corners with newer subdivisions pushing outward toward Sunrise Boulevard and beyond. That mix means our crews handle a real range of work here, from mature oaks and pines that have stood for decades near the older commercial core to younger, still-developing trees in newer developments where good early structural pruning pays off for years to come.

Sacramento County's terrain includes a good amount of wooded land, and Citrus Heights's continued residential growth has kept our land-clearing and lot-preparation crews busy as new construction moves into previously forested areas. Arcade Creek and its tributaries also mean some properties deal with wetter soil conditions than you'd find further into Sacramento proper, which we factor into root health assessments and can affect how a tree responds to drought or disease over time.

We serve all of Citrus Heights and the surrounding communities of Fair Oaks, Carmichael, and Orangevale, giving us a strong presence throughout Sacramento County alongside our core Sacramento metro coverage.
