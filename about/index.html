About Tree Certified DLS Xpert

A locally owned crew that's been climbing Sacramento's trees, and answering its phones, for more than 15 years.

Tree Certified DLS Xpert started with two people, one truck, and a chainsaw, answering calls after a Sacramento Valley windstorm knocked out power for a week when bigger companies weren't picking up. Word spread the way it does in a close-knit metro: neighbors telling neighbors that this crew actually showed up when they said they would, explained the work honestly, and didn't try to sell a removal when a tree could have been saved. Fifteen years later, that's still the reputation we work to protect on every job.

What We Believe

We think a tree company should treat every yard like it's the last job that matters, not a stop on the way to the next one. That means giving honest assessments, even when the honest answer is "you don't need us for this." It means showing up when we say we will, and it means standing behind the work after the truck leaves. The Sacramento Valley's tree canopy took decades to grow, and we don't take removal recommendations lightly — every arborist on our crew has been trained to look for ways to save a tree before reaching for a chainsaw.

Licensed, Insured, and Locally Rooted

Tree Certified DLS Xpert is fully licensed and carries general liability and workers' compensation insurance, and we're happy to provide documentation before any job begins. Every member of our crew lives in the Sacramento metro, which means the trees we work on are often just down the street from our own homes. We know the neighborhoods, the soil, the storm patterns, and the permit requirements across Sacramento, West Sacramento, Elk Grove, Citrus Heights, Rancho Cordova, and the surrounding communities, because we've been working in them for well over a decade.

Tree Certified DLS Xpert is a second generation family business that takes pride in making customers feel well taken care of. We provide service for a wide variety of trees, and go above and beyond your expectations to do the job as exceptionally as possible. Call or text to book your appointment!
